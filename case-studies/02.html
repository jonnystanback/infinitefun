This was their original brand. Built around an EP logo, and bright blue and green for the brand colors. They wanted something stronger and authoritative, that would position them as a higher-tier brand.

Build a bold, iconic brand, with motifs that can be used throughout the product catalog. We developed the hexagon motif to be used for their logo, their paddle, and their ‘targets’ for their practice board.
